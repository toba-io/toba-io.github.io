Background

Understanding systems.
Building useful things.

My training began in chemical engineering, with an interest in how physical processes can be modelled, measured and improved. My undergraduate project explored adaptive model predictive control of an exothermic stirred-tank reactor using MATLAB and Simulink.

That interest now takes me into electrochemical energy storage. Alongside modelling and data analysis, I work with laboratory hardware: cell design, fabrication, assembly, instrumentation and testing. I also co-found Reclaimer Labs, where we are developing MarginPilot for marketplace sellers.

Research experience

From models
to the laboratory.

At Skoltech’s Flow Battery Lab, I built a zero-dimensional vanadium flow-battery digital twin in MATLAB/Simulink and fitted it to laboratory measurements. I investigate which physical parameters the data can determine, and compare conventional optimisation with physics-constrained neural identification.

The experimental work includes developing single cells through CAD, fabrication and assembly, building an OCV and colour reader, and analysing cycling and electrolyte measurements. Two studies focus on the membrane comparison and the electrolyte’s oxidation state.
