A little about me

Curious about how
systems work.

My work brings together physical systems and software: a battery on a test bench, a model fitted to its measurements, or a tool that makes an operational decision easier.

I’m in the second year of the MSc in Digital Electrical Energy at Skoltech in Moscow. My thesis focuses on vanadium flow batteries, with Prof. Mikhail Pugach and Prof. Federico Ibanez. Alongside research, I help co-found Reclaimer Labs.
