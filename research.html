Research.

Physical models and laboratory experiments to understand vanadium flow batteries.

MSc Digital Electrical Energy · Skoltech · Supervisors: Prof. Mikhail Pugach & Prof. Federico Ibanez

Main research · In progress

Flow-battery modelling
and diagnostics.

Can physical model parameters reveal what is changing inside a battery? My main research combines parameter identification, physics-constrained neural methods and a controlled three-membrane comparison.

The first Nafion-117 cycling run is complete, with initial voltage, capacity and efficiency analysis. The remaining membrane runs are planned; interpretation of the observed loss is in progress.

Oxidation-state experiment · Analysis in progress

Understanding
the electrolyte.

This study examines how the starting oxidation state of vanadium affects cycling, using electrical measurements, colour sensing, camera monitoring and titration.

The first ten-cycle run is complete. Chemical, optical and volume analysis is in progress; the wider six-condition series is planned.
