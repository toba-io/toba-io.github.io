Projects / Renewable energy

Solar and wind,
working together.

VetroSol combines photovoltaic generation with a vertical-axis wind turbine. The panels also form channels that guide airflow towards the turbine, bringing two energy sources into one layout.

How it works

Sunlight to current.
Airflow to rotation.

Solar cells convert sunlight into electricity. The panel arrangement directs wind towards the central turbine, which drives a generator. A controller, inverter and storage system are selected to match the site’s energy needs.

With friends, I helped install the panels, vertical-axis turbine and monitoring equipment at the Moscow State University facility in Kamshilovka. We are tracking generation over time. The collaboration began during our Sharjah PV-and-storage course project.

The team’s public project site describes CFD modelling and wind-tunnel prototype tests. Development and testing are ongoing. Intended uses include telecoms and monitoring, autonomous lighting and security, tourism sites, and remote buildings.
